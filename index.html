About Our Fellowship

Proclaiming Christ in the Community of Soweto

Evangelical Bible Church (EBC) Soweto is an evangelical church located in the historic and vibrant Soweto area of Katutura, Windhoek. We believe that the Holy Scriptures are the inspired and inerrant Word of God, profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and training in righteousness.

Our weekly gatherings feature reverent congregational praise, heartfelt prayer for our community and nation, and the expository preaching of the Gospel. Whether singing in English or vernacular choruses, we are united by one Lord, one faith, and one baptism.

First Time in Soweto?

What to Expect on Your Visit

We know visiting a new church can be unfamiliar. At Evangelical Bible Church Soweto, we welcome you with open arms.

What Should I Wear?

You are welcome just as you are. Some members wear traditional dress, others wear smart casual or Sunday attire.

Languages Spoken

Services are conducted with English and vernacular songs/fellowship (including Otjiherero). Everyone can participate comfortably.

Welcoming Ushers

Our ushers at the courtyard entrance will greet you, help you find seating, and answer any questions.

Let Us Welcome You

Let us know when you are planning to visit so we can prepare a friendly welcome!
